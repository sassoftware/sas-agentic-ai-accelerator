## Description

Clef-flash by Cloudflare - an open (Apache-2.0) System One decision model built on Qwen that answers typed questions about a state with calibrated probabilities; the most accurate hosted choice for many-option intent sets, served through the OpenRouter API.

## Evaluation

Accuracy 0.95 on 41 hand-written cases, 0.89 on public datasets (macro-F1 1.00 on BANKING77 and 0.95 on CLINC150 - the best measured on many-option intent sets) and 0.83 on the 231 public JevBench tasks.
