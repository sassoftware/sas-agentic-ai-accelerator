## Deployment

The model runs as a SAS Container Runtime (SCR) image published from SAS Model Manager and is called through its SCR endpoint. What the container needs from its environment:

- The key arrives per call in the `API_KEY` option.
- The container keeps the System One contract for its callers and translates it for Google's Decisions API: the state is sent as the `input` text, the questions unchanged, the key in the `x-goog-api-key` header. It needs network access to `generativelanguage.googleapis.com`.
