SAS Agentic AI Accelerator

The accelerator brings large language models, embedding models and decision models into SAS Viya as models of SAS Model Manager that are published like any other model. With the Prompt Builder you write prompt templates, decision templates and RAG setups, test them against those models and register the result. The log of the containers becomes tables that say which model answered which request, for which prompt, and what it cost.
